    How to play

    🚦 Red lightStop behind the white line. You get an AI question. A right answer earns a permit credit.
    🟢 Green lightDrive through. Yellow means stop if you safely can.
    🛑 Stop signCome to a full stop at the line to collect a Road Note about AI. Rolling through misses it.
    ➤ Blue arrowsFollow them to your practice-hour destination. Directions show at the top.
    ⏱ Practice hoursEach destination logs one hour. The day turns to sunset as you log all six.
    🎓 FinishEarn 3 credits in each category (12 total), then park in the gold circle at the Permit Office.
    🛡️ Safe drivingSpeeding, running lights, rolling stops, the wrong lane and bumps cost points. Clean driving earns them back.
    🎯 Perfect stopsStop within a car length of the white line for a perfect stop.
    ↺ Stuck?Press R (or the ↺ button) to put the car back on the road. No points lost.
    🔊 Read aloudEvery card has a Read aloud button that reads the question or note to you.
    📱 SteerTilt your phone like a steering wheel. Or choose Touch in Pause and slide your thumb left or right.
    👆 Gas and brakeHold anywhere on the road to go. Lift your finger to brake.
    🚦 Red lightLift at the white line. You get an AI question. A right answer earns a permit credit.
    🛑 Stop signCome to a full stop at the line to collect a Road Note about AI. Tap Drive on when you finish reading.
    ➤ Blue arrowsFollow them to each practice-hour destination. The day turns to sunset as you log all six hours.
    🎓 FinishEarn 3 credits in each category (12 total), then park in the gold circle at the Permit Office.
    🛡️ Safe drivingSpeeding, running lights, rolling stops, the wrong lane and bumps cost points. Clean driving earns them back.
    ⏸ PauseYour progress, the town map, turn signals, reverse, Reset car and Recenter tilt are all in Pause.

    Keys: W/↑ gas · S/↓ brake then reverse · A D/← → steer · Space brake · Q/E turn signals · C camera · M map · R reset car · Esc pause
