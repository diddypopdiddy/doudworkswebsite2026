Two levels of readiness for putting the Framework to work.

AI Permit builds foundational readiness with guidance. AI License means independently choosing and sequencing the right support, completing the Production Spiral, and defending the human decisions with Process Evidence. Implementation labs are ways to practice and apply that readiness—not a third level. The Outside Cat stance keeps the goal clear: leverage without dependency.

AI Permit

Session 1 · Earn Your AI Permit

Earn Your AI Permit

The Permit stage builds foundational readiness. Staff learn shared AI literacy, the four always-on Core Competencies, privacy expectations, verification habits, and the five AI Modes with guidance.

  • AI literacy and core competencies
  • Privacy, verification, and responsible use expectations
  • Common language for the five AI Modes

AI License

Session 2 · AI Modes in Action

AI Modes in Action

Practice the Modes on a permitted real task. Participants define what remains human, choose and sequence the support that fits, work through the Production Spiral, and keep the human thinking visible through Process Evidence.

  • Idea, Learning, Feedback, Tool, and Practice Mode
  • Mode shifts inside a real AI conversation
  • Full Production Spiral, verification, Process Evidence, and reflection

Applied practice

Session 3 · Choose a Lab Variant

Choose Your Implementation Lab

The applied session splits into two variants. Choose the workflow lab for teacher and staff systems, or the classroom lab for a student-facing rollout map. Both labs are delivery experiences for demonstrating and extending License-level readiness.

  • Workflow variant: Tool Mode, built-in tools, and practical systems
  • Classroom variant: activities, routines, and student-facing resources
  • Evidence, reflection, and repeatable implementation habits

AI Permit and AI License describe demonstrated levels of readiness for applying the AI Production Framework. They become formal credentials only when a school or program adopts defined requirements and assessment.
