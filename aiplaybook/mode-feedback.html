Feedback Mode in the Framework

Goal

Get critique on work you’ve developed while keeping the judgment and revision with you.

  1. Set upBring your own work
  2. Use AISet criteria and limits
  3. Think it throughEvaluate the suggestions
  4. The human finishRevise, or justify keeping it

Learning objectives

  1. Students will be able to supply their own work, its purpose and the relevant criteria.
  2. Students will be able to request feedback about strengths and gaps, with reasons.
  3. Students will be able to evaluate advice against the brief and approved sources.
  4. Students will be able to make and explain their own revision or justify keeping sound work.

Key vocabulary

Feedback Mode
Get critique on work you’ve developed while keeping the judgment and revision with you.
Criterion
A standard you use to judge how well the work meets its purpose.
Revision
A change you make to improve your own work.
The human finish
The decision, explanation, check or approval you do yourself before using the result.

Task ideas by subject
  • English language artsGet feedback on a draft

    Ask about one part of the rubric. Accept, reject or revise each suggestion in your own words.

    Bring A complete student-written first draft and the rubric.

  • HistoryTest a claim against the evidence

    Share your claim and the evidence you chose. Ask where the evidence falls short and what a historian might challenge. No rewriting.

    Bring Your written claim, your selected sources and the rubric.

  • MathematicsFind the step that went wrong

    Share your full worked solution. Ask it to point to the first step where the reasoning breaks, without giving the answer. Fix it yourself.

    Bring Complete shown work. AI can be wrong about which step is wrong, so recalculate.

  • ScienceCheck a lab conclusion

    Share your claim, evidence and reasoning draft. Ask where the reasoning doesn't follow from the data. Revise it yourself. AI never supplies data.

    Bring Your draft conclusion, your real data and the rubric.

  • World languagesOne correction at a time

    Ask for one explained correction to a sentence you wrote. Not a full rewrite.

    Bring Your own attempt and one focus, such as tense, tone or word order.

  • Business and computer sciencePressure-check a pitch or plan

    Share your draft pitch, plan or pseudocode with the rubric. Ask for two gaps and the effect of each. No rewrite. You decide what changes.

    Bring A finished first draft and the criteria.

  • Physical education and healthCheck a plan against the criteria

    Share your written practice plan or routine design. Ask for gaps against the criteria. Your teacher approves any change before you try it.

    Bring The student's written plan and teacher-approved criteria.

  • Related artsReflect on a work in progress

    Ask questions about intention, craft, audience or alternatives. Not for a replacement idea or an artist statement.

    Bring An existing sketch, draft, rehearsal, composition or object.

Special education access notes and adult-use ideas

Lesson flow

The six steps of guided practice, and what Feedback Mode changes in four of them.

  1. TeacherDesignPick the AI Mode and the task.
  2. TeacherModelShow how you direct the help.
  3. TeacherSupplyHand out a starter prompt.
  4. StudentsEngageDirect the conversation and do the work.
  5. StudentsShareHand in the full, dedicated task conversation.
  6. TeacherReviewRead, discuss and record the mark.

Design · students bring

Your own first attempt or draft, and two criteria from the assignment.

Model · show this

Show an approved draft and one criterion. Read a suggestion, compare it with the criterion and write or decline the change yourself.

Say thisI asked for the gap and a reason. I’ll decide what to change and write it myself.

Engage · students do this

Ask for specific gaps with reasons. Check the advice against your brief and sources. Write your own changes and explain one you made or chose to skip.

Exit · ask this

AskWhich suggestion improved the draft, or why did you keep what you had?

Supply, Share and Review work the same way in every AI Mode. See all six steps, or print the full plan for this session.

With guidance means a cue identifies the decision the student needs to make. Record the exact cue. Permitted AI help can still be part of independent work. The student must do the thinking being assessed.

Read the evidence in the conversation. Length, spelling, polish and AI praise do not show readiness. You give the mark and record the level.
