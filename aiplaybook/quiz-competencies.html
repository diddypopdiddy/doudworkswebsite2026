Quiz

The four AI Core Competencies

About 12–15 minutes. Use short answers. Where a question asks for two Core Competencies, give an action for each.

Name ________________________Date ____________

  1. Your group needs two layout options for a display. The class rules reserve the wording and layout choice for you. Write one prompt that gives AI a useful job.

  2. You ask a tool to pose one rehearsal question. It gives you the whole answer. What is your next message?

  3. You ask “Help me with my presentation.” Your audience is new to the topic and you only want ideas for the opening. Name two details that would make the request fit.

  4. An approved tool may sort sample supply lists. Your real list contains a classmate’s phone number and notes about who borrows supplies. What should you give the tool?

  5. Your school approved a chat tool for class. You can’t tell if it approved the new file-upload feature. What do you do before uploading?

  6. The directions allow AI to format a table. The tool offers to write your conclusion too. What should you do?

  7. You kept two suggestions from AI feedback but wrote your own revision. Write one sentence that tells the teacher how you used it.

  8. The supplied schedule says the event starts at 4:00. AI writes that it starts at 4:30. Classify the claim and say what to do.

  9. A floor plan marks two entrances. AI claims both have automatic doors. The plan shows no door details. Classify the claim and say what to do.

  10. The original inventory lists three boxes of four brushes. An AI table says 12 brushes total. Classify the total and show the check.

  11. You’re allowed to get feedback on a club poster. The draft includes someone’s private email, and the tool asks to rewrite the whole poster. Name two Core Competencies that apply and one action for each.

  12. AI gives you an unlisted cost and a finished recommendation after you asked for budget feedback only. Name two Core Competencies that apply and one action for each.
