Ground rules

Before you plan anything

Students still

Students make the artistic choices, practice the craft, respond to critique and create the final work.

Road closed when

The goal is an original point of view, hands-on technique, live performance or a first creative response.

Check it by

Check reliable arts sources and have the student explain the intention, process and material choices.

Have students keep

The full conversation, plus sketches, drafts, rehearsals, process photos, critique notes and the artist's own reflection.

Watch for: AI can imitate styles, blur authorship and flatten cultural context.

Watch for: Finished-looking output can hide the practice the assignment is supposed to build.

Guided practice · Module 3

One idea for each Mode

Each one is a single guided-practice cycle. Adapt the task to your unit.

Idea ModeStudent useNew

Find a direction

Surface possible themes, questions, audiences or limits. Then transform one through sketching, rehearsal or making.

BringThe student's interests, references, first point of view or exploratory work.

Learning ModeStudent useNew

Learn about a technique or context

Ask about a technique, material or historical context, then check the answer against reliable arts sources.

BringA specific work, technique, artist or tradition and a source to check against.

Feedback ModeStudent useNew

Reflect on a work in progress

Ask questions about intention, craft, audience or alternatives. Not for a replacement idea or an artist statement.

BringAn existing sketch, draft, rehearsal, composition or object.

Practice ModeStudent useNew

Rehearse the critique

Have AI play an audience member asking one question at a time about your intention and choices. Answer before it responds. It isn't the judge of quality.

BringYour work in progress described in your own words, and your intention.

Tool ModeStudent useNew

Plan the logistics

Turn your own rehearsal notes, materials list or shot list into a schedule or checklist. Compare it with your notes. No creative decisions handed over.

BringYour notes and the format you want.

Suggestions to adapt for your unit. Give students a fresh assessment task after any guided rehearsal.

Suggested worked brief

Artist statement and process board

Task
For a piece you made this term, write a 150-word artist statement and build a process board showing how the work developed.
Only source
Use only your approved process notes, your own sketches or images, and the class vocabulary. Sample process notes for a fictional collage: first sketch used three similar shapes. The second grouped two shapes, added a large empty area and changed the brightest color to blue. The final uses a narrow dark strip as the focal point.
What AI is permitted to do
Use the approved tool and source. Use and document all five Modes in the order that fits your task. The options below show where help could fit. Choose your own prompts.
  • Idea ModeCompare ways to frame the piece.
  • Learning ModePin down a term or a piece of context.
  • Feedback ModeCheck that the statement matches what's visible in the work.
  • Practice ModeRehearse the critique.
  • Tool ModeBuild the caption list from your own notes.
What stays yours
The work itself, the intention and every sentence of the statement.
Subject learning assessed separately
Specific connection between the intention, visible choices and a change made during the process. The board should show how the work developed.
What to hand in
The finished work, the full, dedicated task conversation, and a short explanation of what each Mode contributed, what you accepted or rejected, and what you verified.
Privacy
Use materials you have permission to share. Keep photos of people and identifiable work by classmates out of the tool.
Fresh transfer task
A short response to a work the student hasn't seen.

Another starting point

The program note

Source
Class materials on the piece.
What stays with the student
The interpretation, the performance choices and the text.
