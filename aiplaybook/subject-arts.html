Ground rules

Before you plan anything

Students still

Students make the artistic choices, practice the craft, respond to critique and create the final work.

Road closed when

The goal is an original point of view, hands-on technique, live performance or a first creative response.

Check it by

Check reliable arts sources and have the student explain the intention, process and material choices.

Have students keep

The full conversation, plus sketches, drafts, rehearsals, process photos, critique notes and the artist's own reflection.

Watch for: AI can imitate styles, blur authorship and flatten cultural context.

Watch for: Finished-looking output can hide the practice the assignment is supposed to build.

Practice ModeStudent useNew

Rehearse the critique

Have AI play an audience member asking one question at a time about your intention and choices. Answer before it responds. It isn't the judge of quality.

BringYour work in progress described in your own words, and your intention.

Plan the logistics

Suggestion · Worked brief

Artist statement and process board

Task
For a piece you made this term, write a 150-word artist statement and build a process board showing how the work developed.
Your only source
Use the piece you made, your approved process notes and class vocabulary. In this example, the notes record three versions of a paper collage: the first had evenly spaced shapes, the second cropped one shape at the edge, and the final repeated a blue shape to lead the eye toward the lower right. Use only your own process evidence.
AI is permitted for
  • IdeaCompare ways to frame the piece.
  • LearningPin down a term or a piece of context.
  • FeedbackCheck that the statement matches what's visible in the work.
  • PracticeRehearse the critique.
  • ToolBuild the caption list from your own notes.

Use each AI Mode purposefully. Choose your own prompts and order.

Yours and assessed
Choose what to show and explain how your artistic choices changed the work. Write every sentence yourself. Check the statement against the work and process record.
Assessed separately
Related arts: connection between intention and decisions, accurate process evidence and clear use of art vocabulary. Review responsible AI use separately.
Hand in
A 150-word artist statement and a process board with your own captions, the full, dedicated task conversation, and an explanation of what you accepted or rejected, what you checked and how you developed the work.
Privacy
Use approved task material and your own work. Keep names, identifiable images and personal information out of the tool. Submit through your private class route.
