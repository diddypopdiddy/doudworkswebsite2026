Learning Mode

Printable lesson plan

Goal Use explanations, questions and hints to build understanding you can explain, check and apply yourself.

Learning objectives

  1. Students will be able to describe what they know, where they’re stuck and the approved context.
  2. Students will be able to choose a fitting explanation, hint, example or question.
  3. Students will be able to restate what they learned and ask a follow-up about a remaining gap.
  4. Students will be able to check their understanding against a trusted source and apply it in a fresh example.

Key vocabulary

Learning Mode · Learning gap · Paraphrase · The human finish

Materials

Student starter prompt for Learning Mode · Five Mode Studio Student Workbook, Learning section, pp. 11–19 · An approved source and the task criteria

Student starter prompt I'm stuck on [concept]. Here's what I think I know: [your words]. Give me one hint or example at a time, then ask me to explain it back.

TimeStepTeacherStudentsSay this
Before class · 5 min in classOpening
Design		Choose a task for this Mode. State what Learning Mode may do, what students must do and which source is approved.Name the task’s limits. Bring: Your first attempt or a specific question, plus an approved class source.“Today you’ll use Learning Mode. Read the task and point to the thinking that stays with you.”
5 minTeach
Model		Use your saved conversation or the Framework example. Share a partial understanding. Ask for one hint, restate the idea and check it against the class source before trying a new example.Name one point where the model supported the task and one decision the teacher kept.“Here’s what I think I know. Give me one hint, then wait while I explain it back.”
2 minTeach
Supply		Hand out the starter prompt, the approved source and the task criteria.Fill in the brackets. Check that the information you share is minimal and approved.“Give it enough context to do the permitted job. What can you leave out?”
20 minPractice
Engage		Circulate. Ask what the learner is keeping, changing or checking. Record the exact wording of a cue that identifies a needed decision.Ask about your gap, paraphrase the response, and follow up. Close or cover the chat and explain a new example using the class source to verify.“Point to the part where you directed the help or decided what to do with the response.”
3 minCheck
Share		Use the approved conversation hand-in and confirm the evidence arrives privately.Turn in the task work and the full, dedicated task conversation.“Open what you handed in. Check that the first prompt, every response and your final turn are there.”
5 min · finish reviewing after classCheck
Review		Use the Learning Mode rubric to review the conversation. Mark the finished cycle, record the observed level and give a specific next action.Explain a key decision and point to the turn or class source that supports it.“Show me the evidence of what you checked and what you chose.”
5 minExit
Exit with the human finish		Collect a one-sentence explanation and use it alongside the work and conversation to plan a next attempt.Write a brief answer pointing to your own work.“What can you explain without the chat open? Which source helped you check it?”

Check for understanding Learning Mode rubric, a separate printable. Record the level and the exact cue, if you gave one.

Misconceptions and reteach

  • “It said I got it, so I understand.” Say: “Show your understanding in your own explanation and check it with the source.” Redo: Close the chat and apply the idea to a different class example.
