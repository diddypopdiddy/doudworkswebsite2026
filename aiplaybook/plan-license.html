AI Educator Playbook

The License assessment

Printable lesson plan

Goal Students show they can use AI independently on an open task.

Learning objectives

  1. Students will be able to choose and document a purpose for all five AI Modes within an open-ended task.
  2. Students will be able to apply the four AI Core Competencies while keeping the subject thinking and the human finish their own.
  3. Students will be able to explain what they accepted, rejected and verified using evidence from the work and conversation.
  4. Students will be able to show how they would apply the same skills to a fresh transfer task.

Key vocabulary

License · Purposeful Mode use · The full, dedicated task conversation · Fresh transfer task

Materials

Permit and License slide deck · Workbook Chapter 3 · Final Boss · Independent AI User License Student Packet · Task brief template

TimeStepTeacherStudentsSay this
5 minOpening
Launch the task		Hand out a fresh, open-ended brief and one approved source. State the assessed thinking, permitted actions, privacy limits and approved hand-in method.Read the brief and explain one decision the task reserves for you.“Use every Mode for a reason. You choose the order and write your own prompts.”
5 minTeach
Clarify the directions		Answer procedural questions and provide needed access supports such as read-aloud directions, larger text, a scribe or extra time. Record any coaching that identifies a content decision.Identify the work you’ll produce and the evidence you’ll submit. Ask about any unclear permission.“What’s your source? What do you need permission to share?”
25–45 minPractice
Complete the open task		Observe. Keep the assessed decisions with the student. If you give a content cue, record exactly what it was.Use all five Modes within the task’s limits. Check material claims and operations against your source. Keep the whole conversation and finish the work yourself.“Keep a record of how each Mode helped and which decisions you made.”
5 minCheck
Collect the work and conversation		Use your approved private hand-in route. Check that the conversation runs from the opening prompt through the human finish.Submit the finished work, the full, dedicated task conversation and your explanation of what you accepted, rejected and checked.“Show me enough of your process to explain how you got to the finished work.”
About 20 minCheck
Give the fresh transfer task		Use a new brief and approved source during class. Ask the student to explain which Modes fit and have them complete one live. Keep the case for teacher use.Explain which Modes would help and why. Use one and show what you checked.“What would you do here? Which decision stays with you?”
5 minExit
Explain a decision from the work		Ask each learner to name a purposeful use and one piece of evidence for it. Review the rubric and record your decision after you have all the evidence.Point to a turn where you accepted, rejected, redirected or verified something. Explain why.“Find a choice you made and show how it affected what you turned in.”

Check for understanding The License rubric (LT-04 and LT-05 in the classroom kit), then your decision: Ready, Needs another attempt or Pause.

Misconceptions and reteach

  • “I need five Modes, so I’ll add one extra prompt for each.” Say: “Each Mode needs a real use. What did it help you do, and what did you decide?” Redo: Revisit the missing or unconvincing use on a fresh task.
  • “The finished work is strong, so I’m ready.” Say: “I’ll review the subject work and your AI use separately. Show me the choices and checks in the conversation.” Redo: Ask the student to explain one choice and verify it using a new source.
  • “Read-aloud directions count as coaching.” Say: “Access supports help you reach the same target. A content cue supplies part of a decision.” Redo: Record the support separately and gather fresh independent evidence if a content cue was needed.
