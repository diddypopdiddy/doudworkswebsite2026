Special education lens.

Carefully bounded adult use. No private information and no individualized decisions handed to AI.

Ground rules

Before anything goes in

Educators still

Educators and teams make every individualized decision. Student and family voice, professional judgment and direct learner evidence stay central.

Road closed when

The work involves eligibility, goals, placement, accommodations, services, grades, discipline or any other high-stakes decision.

Check it by

Use the accountable team process, approved sources, direct learner evidence and ongoing monitoring.

Keep

Direct learner evidence, team observations, approved plans, review notes and the reason a support was chosen.

Watch for: Never enter identifiable student, medical, disability, behavior, grade or IEP information into a public AI tool.

Watch for: AI can repeat stereotypes, lower expectations or make a generic suggestion sound individualized.

Adult use only

One idea for each AI Mode

These are for educators, not students. Every one starts from a fictional or fully de-identified context.

Idea ModeTeacher useNew

Brainstorm possible supports

Create a menu of possible supports for an adult team to evaluate. Not a recommendation for a named student.

BringA clear learning goal, direct evidence about the task and a fully de-identified or fictional context.

Learning ModeTeacher useNew

Get up to speed on a support

Ask for a plain explanation of an unfamiliar strategy, term or assistive technology. Check it against trusted professional sources before it reaches the team.

BringThe term or strategy and a trusted source. No student details.

Feedback ModeTeacher useNew

Check an access plan for barriers

Ask what barriers, stereotypes, hidden assumptions or practical problems an adult-created plan may still contain.

BringA team-created draft, de-identified context, applicable policy and direct learner evidence.

Practice ModeTeacher useNew

Rehearse a hard conversation

Rehearse explaining a plan or a difficult update using an entirely made-up family scenario. Try it first, then ask for feedback on clarity and tone.

BringA fictional scenario and your key points.

Tool ModeTeacher useNew

Rewrite an explanation in plainer language

Turn approved information into a clearer explanation or another format, for professional review.

BringAccurate source text, the intended audience, access needs and an adult who checks every change.

License · Module 4

Access on the License

Support that keeps the target intact is access. Support that names the answer is coaching. Record which one happened.

Keeps the target intact

  • Oral responses and read-aloud directions.
  • Larger text or a scribe.
  • Translated directions.
  • Extra time.

Changes the evidence

A hint that names the answer changes the evidence to "with guidance". Record access support separately from answer coaching.
