Module 5 · Each assignment

Using the License

A suggested pace. Stretch or combine days to fit your class.

At a glance
Time
As you post each assignment

Goal

State the assignment’s AI permission and boundaries so students can explain them before they start.

Set the assignment conditions

Keep the teacher’s authority over each assignment, including for students with a License.

Teacher allows AI

Name the permitted actions and what students must do themselves. You can permit one Mode for one purpose. License holders can choose and combine Modes within the actions you approve.

Say thisUse Feedback Mode on your own draft. Ask about evidence and clarity. Make every revision yourself. Hand in the draft, final and the full task conversation.

Pulled over

Pause the task to review the conversation, process or an explanation. Tell students in advance to expect this kind of check.

Say thisPause here and show me how you got to that decision. Give me the conversation and your work so far.

Road closed

Set the assignment to No AI, including for License holders. Use this when you need to see unaided recall, fluency, first-draft voice or a live performance.

Say thisRoad closed. Do the brainstorming, drafting and checking on your own today.

Before you post

  1. What learning will I assess?
  2. Which AI actions, if any, are allowed?
  3. Which decisions and work must students own?
  4. What goes in the submission and where does it go?
  5. What accessible or non-AI route can serve the same learning goal?

Assess the work and the AI use

Subject learning

Apply your ordinary subject criteria. Ask for a short independent explanation or demonstration when the evidence needs it.

Responsible AI use

Check the full conversation for the requested help, permission, privacy, verification and what the student chose. Follow any required citation style and collect the full task conversation.
