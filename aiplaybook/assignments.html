Using the License in the Framework

Goal

Give every assignment a clear rule students hear before they start.

Set the rule for the assignment

Tell students which AI actions the task allows. A License stays under the teacher’s direction.

Teacher allows AI

Permit one AI Mode for one purpose when only one kind of help fits or the class is in guided practice. For instance, allow Feedback on a finished first draft and ask for the draft, final and full conversation.

When the task has room for different kinds of help, License holders can choose and combine AI Modes within the supporting actions you permit. State what thinking stays with students. Collect the work, the full conversation and a note about what they accepted, rejected and checked.

On the assignment sheetIn this assignment you may get Feedback on your own draft. Write the revision yourself and hand in the draft, final and full conversation.

Pulled over

Pause the work to check the conversation, process or explanation. Make these checks routine, including when there’s no concern.

On the assignment sheetI may ask you to show the full conversation and explain a choice you made.

Road closed

Complete this activity without AI even if you hold a License. Close it when you need to see what students can do alone, or when private information is involved.

  • Independent recall, fluency or first-draft voice.
  • Live performance or discussion.
On the assignment sheetRoad closed. Do this on your own. That includes the brainstorming and checking.

Before you post it

Identify the thinking you are assessing and make the assignment’s limits clear.

  1. What thinking am I assessing?
  2. Does the assignment say Teacher allows AI or Road closed? Have I explained how a Pulled over check works?
  3. If AI is allowed, exactly what may it do?
  4. What do students hand in besides the work?
  5. What's the route for a student who can't use AI?

Grade subject learning and review AI use

The subject learning

Did they learn what the assignment was for? Grade this the way you always have. Polished doesn't mean learned.

The AI use

Did the help fit the task? Did they redirect, protect information, check what mattered and explain their decisions? Give credit for that, not for a slick output.

Build in a moment to talk

A checkpoint, a two-minute conference or a quick explanation at the board. "Explain it without the chatbot" tells you more than any written note.

If a style guide wants a citation

Follow it. A formal citation doesn't replace the full conversation, though. It says AI was used, not how.
