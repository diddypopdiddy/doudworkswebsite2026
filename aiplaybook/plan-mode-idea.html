AI Production Teacher Playbook

Idea Mode

Printable lesson plan

Goal Explore alternatives for a real task, then choose or adapt a direction and explain why it fits.

Learning objectives

  1. Students will be able to bring their own starting thought or question, audience and constraints.
  2. Students will be able to ask for distinct possibilities and tradeoffs within the task’s limits.
  3. Students will be able to question, refine, combine or reject alternatives.
  4. Students will be able to explain where their final idea came from and why it fits.

Key vocabulary

Idea Mode · Alternative · Tradeoff · The human finish

Materials

Student starter prompt for Idea Mode · Five Mode Studio Student Workbook, Idea section, pp. 2–10 · An approved source and the task criteria

Student starter prompt I'm working on [task]. My starting idea is [idea]. Give me three genuinely different directions and one tradeoff for each. Don't write it for me.

TimeStepTeacherStudentsSay this
Before class · 5 min in classOpening
Design		Choose a task for this Mode. State what Idea Mode may do, what students must do and which source is approved.Name the task’s limits. Bring: A starting idea or question, the task goal, audience and constraints.“Today you’ll use Idea Mode. Read the task and point to the thinking that stays with you.”
5 minTeach
Model		Use your saved conversation or the Framework example. Compare two suggested directions with a constraint from your brief. Explain why you reject one, then show how you adapt the other.Name one point where the model supported the task and one decision the teacher kept.“This one fits the time limit. What would I have to change to make it work for our audience?”
2 minTeach
Supply		Hand out the starter prompt, the approved source and the task criteria.Fill in the brackets. Check that the information you share is minimal and approved.“Give it enough context to do the permitted job. What can you leave out?”
20 minPractice
Engage		Circulate. Ask what the learner is keeping, changing or checking. Record the exact wording of a cue that identifies a needed decision.Ask for choices and one tradeoff each. Compare them against a class constraint, redirect when needed, and explain your final choice in your own words.“Point to the part where you directed the help or decided what to do with the response.”
3 minCheck
Share		Use the approved conversation hand-in and confirm the evidence arrives privately.Turn in the task work and the full, dedicated task conversation.“Open what you handed in. Check that the first prompt, every response and your final turn are there.”
5 min · finish reviewing after classCheck
Review		Use the Idea Mode rubric to review the conversation. Mark the finished cycle, record the observed level and give a specific next action.Explain a key decision and point to the turn or class source that supports it.“Show me the evidence of what you checked and what you chose.”
5 minExit
Exit with the human finish		Collect a one-sentence explanation and use it alongside the work and conversation to plan a next attempt.Write a brief answer pointing to your own work.“Which idea did you keep, change or reject? Name the constraint that guided you.”

Check for understanding Idea Mode rubric, a separate printable. Record the level and the exact cue, if you gave one.

Misconceptions and reteach

  • “I picked the one AI ranked first.” Say: “Compare your options with your goal and audience. You make the choice.” Redo: Compare two options with one task constraint and explain your choice aloud.
