Feedback Mode · Guided practice rubric

Record the evidence and support for one practice session.

Read the finished work and the full, dedicated task conversation. Record the human finish and any exact cue you give. With guidance means a cue from you identified the choice or check the student needed. Score the evidence of the student’s decisions. Chat length, polish and AI praise carry no weight.
