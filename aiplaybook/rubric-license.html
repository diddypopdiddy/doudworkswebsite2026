FeedbackNot yetNo human-developed work or criteria; follows suggestions or substitutes a rewrite without evaluation.With guidanceAfter a criterion/source cue, checks advice and makes/explains a revision or justified retention.IndependentSupplies own developed work, criteria, and boundaries; evaluates advice; authors a justified revision or retains adequate work with evidence.

ToolNot yetVague direction; accepts a fabricated value or changed meaning; no verified approval before use.With guidanceAfter a preservation/check cue, repairs the operation and tests the result before approving it.IndependentDefines task/result, directs within limits, checks/refines when needed, and verifies/approves what will be used.

The four AI Core Competencies

CriterionNot yetWith guidanceIndependent
PromptingNot yetRequests a finished answer or misfitting help and cannot explain a needed change.With guidanceAfter a content cue, supplies the goal/context/boundary or repairs the requested role.IndependentDirects fitting help using goal, audience, allowed context and constraints; tests the response and adjusts the request when needed.
Data & PrivacyNot yetProposes or shares private, identifying or unnecessary sensitive data; assumes names alone make it safe.With guidanceAfter a cue, removes risky data, selects a minimal approved substitute or seeks permission.IndependentScreens input before use; shares only minimal approved material; uses safe substitutes and stops to ask when sensitivity or permission is uncertain.
EthicsNot yetCrosses permission limits, hides material assistance or cannot explain ownership and assigned learning.With guidanceAfter a cue, follows limits, describes assistance or explains/revises a choice.IndependentChecks permission, follows scope, discloses material help, owns decisions and independently explains or performs the assigned learning.
VerificationNot yetAccepts a material unsupported claim, invented source or incorrect operation; cannot show a check.With guidanceAfter an instructional cue, uses a relevant source/test and repairs or withholds the material output.IndependentChecks material factual claims, source support, task fit and operations; distinguishes supported, contradicted and not established; corrects or withholds unsupported content.
