Mathematics

Quick ideas for using AI in math class—without letting it do the thinking.

Jump to
Ideas to tryWhat students still doWatch outQuick check
01

Ideas to try

Student useLearning Mode

Get a second explanation

Ask for a visual, a simpler explanation, or a similar example. Compare it with the class method.

Start hereA real first attempt and the exact point of confusion.

Student usePractice Mode

Challenge the reasoning

Ask for a counterexample or challenge question, then defend or revise the original method.

Start hereA student-chosen method, shown work, and an explanation of why it should work.

Teacher useTool Mode

Make extra practice

Create similar practice problems, then solve and check every one before students see them.

Start hereThe concept, difficulty, access needs, and one verified example.

02

What students still do

Students choose the method, show the work, check the answer, and explain why it makes sense.

03

Watch out

  • AI can produce confident-looking math that is completely wrong.
  • A polished answer is not proof that the student understands it.
  • Skip AI when: you need to see what a student can solve independently.
04

Quick check

  • Recalculate. Try another method. Check the units. Have the student explain the reasoning without AI.
  • Save: the original attempt, shown work, corrections, and the final explanation.
