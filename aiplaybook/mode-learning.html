Learning Mode in the Framework

Goal

Use explanations, questions and hints to build understanding you can explain, check and apply yourself.

  1. Set upState what you know and your goal
  2. Use AIAsk for tailored explanations
  3. Think it throughAsk clarifying or deeper questions
  4. The human finishShow and check your understanding

Learning objectives

  1. Students will be able to describe what they know, where they’re stuck and the approved context.
  2. Students will be able to choose a fitting explanation, hint, example or question.
  3. Students will be able to restate what they learned and ask a follow-up about a remaining gap.
  4. Students will be able to check their understanding against a trusted source and apply it in a fresh example.

Key vocabulary

Learning Mode
Use explanations, questions and hints to build understanding you can explain, check and apply yourself.
Learning gap
The part you can’t explain or do yet.
Paraphrase
Explain the idea in your own words while preserving its meaning.
The human finish
The decision, explanation, check or approval you do yourself before using the result.

Task ideas by subject
  • English language artsUnpack a hard word or passage

    Ask for a simpler explanation or a comparison, say it back in your own words, then return to the assigned text.

    Bring The assigned text, a first attempt at reading it and class materials to check against.

  • HistoryClarify a concept

    Ask for another explanation or a comparison, say it back, then check it against course materials and the historical context.

    Bring A first attempt at explaining it and course sources to check against.

  • MathematicsGet a second explanation

    Ask for a visual, a simpler explanation or a similar example. Compare it with the class method.

    Bring A real first attempt and the exact point of confusion.

  • ScienceCompare explanations

    Ask for an analogy or another explanation, say it back, then compare it with trusted scientific sources.

    Bring A first attempt at explaining it and the course text or lab evidence.

  • World languagesClarify grammar, tone or vocabulary

    Compare examples or ask for an explanation, then check it against class materials or authentic use.

    Bring A specific sentence or point of confusion and a trusted source to check against.

  • Business and computer scienceUnderstand an error or a concept

    Name the error or concept, say what you think it means and ask for a hint, not a fix. Explain it back and apply it to a fresh example.

    Bring Your own attempt and the exact point of confusion.

  • Physical education and healthUnderstand the why behind a skill

    Ask for a plain explanation of a class concept, say it back, then check it against class materials. For concepts, not personal health questions.

    Bring What you already think and the class resource to check against.

  • Related artsLearn about a technique or context

    Ask about a technique, material or historical context, then check the answer against reliable arts sources.

    Bring A specific work, technique, artist or tradition and a source to check against.

Special education access notes and adult-use ideas

Lesson flow

The six steps of guided practice, and what Learning Mode changes in four of them.

  1. TeacherDesignPick the AI Mode and the task.
  2. TeacherModelShow how you direct the help.
  3. TeacherSupplyHand out a starter prompt.
  4. StudentsEngageDirect the conversation and do the work.
  5. StudentsShareHand in the full, dedicated task conversation.
  6. TeacherReviewRead, discuss and record the mark.

Design · students bring

Your first attempt or a specific question, plus an approved class source.

Model · show this

Share a partial understanding. Ask for one hint, restate the idea and check it against the class source before trying a new example.

Say thisHere’s what I think I know. Give me one hint, then wait while I explain it back.

Engage · students do this

Ask about your gap, paraphrase the response, and follow up. Close or cover the chat and explain a new example using the class source to verify.

Exit · ask this

AskWhat can you explain without the chat open? Which source helped you check it?
