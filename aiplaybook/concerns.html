Module 6 · When needed

When something feels off

A suggested pace. Stretch or combine days to fit your class.

Total time
About ten minutes with the student
Live student tool
None. Use the submitted evidence.
Safeguards in the Framework

Goal

Respond to concerns by reviewing the available evidence with the student.

Review the concern with the student

  1. 1Pulled over

    Ask for the conversation, calmly

    Privately, and in the same tone you'd use for any check.

    Say thisWalk me through how you got here. Can I see the conversation for this one?
  2. 2Look

    Read what you actually have

    The full conversation, drafts, sources and earlier work. Compare them with what the assignment allowed.

    Watch for: Deciding before you've read it. A leap in quality is a reason to look, not a finding.

  3. 3Talk

    Have the student explain the work

    Use the questions below. Give students an accessible way to explain how they made or checked part of the work.

  4. 4Decide

    Name what it is, then choose the next move

    Most of the time it's one of the three things below. Write down the evidence you have, not your hunch.

Questions to ask

  1. What did you ask AI to do on this one?
  2. What did it give you that you changed or threw out?
  3. Explain this paragraph to me in your own words.
  4. How did you check this claim?
  5. If you did it again, what would you do differently?

Follow up on what the evidence shows

A skills gap

They leaned too hard or didn't check. Send the specific gap back for guided practice, then look at fresh evidence.

A broken task rule

AI was used where the road was closed, or the help was hidden. Follow your school's academic integrity process and its due process.

A tool or data problem

The wrong tool, or private information went in. Pause live use and follow school procedure before resuming.

Use the work and the conversation as evidence

Don't treat a detector score as proof

Detector results are uncertain and can be wrong. Never use one as the only evidence, and never as an automatic consequence.

Don't ask AI to judge it

Pasting student work into a chatbot to ask "did AI write this?" proves nothing and shares the student's work. You inspect the evidence and make the call.

Don't assume the worst

A missing conversation is a problem to fix. It doesn't automatically mean misconduct.

Routine checks

Check a few conversations on every assignment that allows AI, including from students who are doing fine.
