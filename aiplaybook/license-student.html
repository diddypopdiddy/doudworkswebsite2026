AI Production Teacher Playbook

Your AI Production License assessment

You’ll make a class project, show how you used AI, and work through two new examples yourself.

Before you open AI

Read your assignment. Check what help your teacher allows and what you can share. Save your first try, rough plan, or one specific question about where you are stuck.

Make the project

  1. Write your own prompts. Use each of the five AI Modes for a job that helps the project. You choose the order.
  2. Check important claims or changes against your class source or a test you can explain. Fix or leave out anything you can’t support.
  3. Make the important choices yourself and finish your class work.

What to hand in

  • Your first try and finished work, along with your sources and what you checked.
  • The whole chat for this assignment, from your first message to the last reply. Use the private place your teacher gives you.
  • A short Evidence Log. For each Mode, point to a message and explain why you used it. Say what you checked and what you kept, changed, or left out.

Do two new checks without AI

  1. Read a new situation about AI. Say what you would do and why.
  2. Read the new example from your class. Solve it, explain it, or show the skill yourself.

Do both checks on your own, without AI. Use the class materials and supports your teacher has approved.
