AI Production Teacher Playbook

AI Literacy

Printable lesson plan

Goal. Explain how AI makes a prediction and use a class source to check an AI answer. Time. Two 45-minute periods

Learning objectives

  • Explain how a model learns patterns from examples.
  • Tell the difference between picking a label and predicting the next piece of text.
  • Place today’s chat tools on a short AI timeline.
  • Use a source to check a chatbot claim. Explain what you would keep or correct.

Materials

  • Pen, mug, and spoon, or pictures of each. Bring a different pen or mug for testing.
  • AI Literacy slides 2–5 and 7 on Day 1. Use slide 6 to open the history activity on Day 2.
  • Print or project the library example below. Use the Quiz Board and the suggested quiz on Day 2.

Teachable Machine demo

  1. On the teacher’s device, open Teachable Machine’s image project. Make two groups named Pen and Mug.
  2. Show a pen and a mug from several angles, one group at a time. Call these examples training data. Then select Train Model.
  3. Before each test, have students guess the label. Try a different pen or mug, then a spoon. Ask students to explain why a model that only knows the two labels still picks one.

Use objects only. Check school approval and the tool’s terms. Use the teacher device without signing in or saving the model. For a paper demo, give pairs labeled pen and mug pictures to study, then ask them to sort a new pen, mug, and spoon into the two groups.

Check a chatbot answer

Class notice. The school library is open until 3:30 on normal school days. On Wednesdays it closes at 2:30.

Chatbot answer. “You can use the library until 3:30 on Wednesday.”

Write your answer. Which part of the chatbot’s answer would you check? Which words in the notice help you? Write the answer you would give a classmate.
