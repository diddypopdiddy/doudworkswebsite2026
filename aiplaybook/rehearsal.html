Module 3 · After the five Mode lessons

Practice a whole project

A suggested pace. Stretch or combine days to fit your class.

MaterialsLesson flowCheck for understanding
At a glance
Time
45–60 minutes, plus feedback and a retry as needed
Live tool
School-approved tool

Goal

Use the five AI Modes together on one practice project and get feedback before the License assessment.

Learning objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. Start with your own work and pick the order that fits your task.
  2. Give each AI Mode a job that helps your project. Make the important choices yourself.
  3. Check an important claim or change and explain how you checked it.
  4. Hand in your work, the whole chat for the assignment, and a short note about your choices.

Key vocabulary

Evidence Log
Short notes that point to decisions and checks in the whole chat.

Materials

  • Classroom kit rehearsal L1-R1 (student packet, p. 3) and the teacher model LT-03 (License Assessment Guide, p. 3).
  • A repeatable practice brief in your subject, approved sources and the usual rules for AI and private submission.
  • The student practice sheet. Use the storyboard example or replace it with a similar brief in your class.
  • Open the student practice sheet

Lesson flow

Schedule this after the five guided Mode lessons and before the License assessment. Demonstrate one switch between Modes and give feedback. Change the brief for the assessment.

  1. 5 min

    Opening

    Teacher. Give the practice brief. Name what AI can help with, what students must do, and which information they may share.

    Students. Write your own first plan or explanation. Mark one place you might want help.

    Say thisStart with your own thinking. What job might you give AI?
  2. 8 min

    Teach

    Teacher. Model a short section of the kit’s R1 rehearsal. Think aloud as you change Modes, check one reply and make a choice. Show what you would write in the Evidence Log.

    Students. Explain why the teacher changed Modes and what the teacher checked before using the reply.

    Say thisWhat changed in the work that made a different kind of help useful?
  3. 22–32 min

    Practice

    Teacher. Have students choose their own prompts and Mode order. Ask why a Mode fits or what they checked. Note any hint that supplied part of a decision.

    Students. Use all five Modes for useful jobs. Check an important claim or change and finish the work yourself.

    Say thisShow a place where you checked, changed, or turned down a reply.
  4. 8–13 min

    Check

    Teacher. Collect the same kinds of work and chat evidence the License requires. Give one next step using the same assessment criteria.

    Students. Hand in your work, the whole chat for this assignment, and your Evidence Log. Point to one decision you made.

    Say thisShow me the part of the work and the message where you made that choice.
  5. 2 min

    Exit

    Teacher. Choose who is ready to try without prompts or answer hints. Give a different practice example where more evidence is needed.

    Students. Write one choice you can handle on your own and one thing you need to practice.

    Say thisWhat will you do on your own when you get a new project?

Check for understanding

Look for a student who can pick the help they need, change course when a reply is off, and show a check without an answer hint.

  • Ask what each Mode did for the project and what the student still did themselves.
  • Give a new practice example if the student needs another try with a specific skill.
  • Keep the actual assessment examples for the day you give the assessment.

Misconceptions and reteach

All five Modes feel forced

Help the student identify a useful job for the missing Mode. Then try a different brief with less help.

The reply sounded right

Ask the student to point to the part of the source or test they used.
