AI Educator Playbook

Practice Mode

Printable lesson plan

Goal Use AI to set up a rehearsal, attempt the skill yourself and use checked feedback to improve.

Learning objectives

  1. Students will be able to set up a scenario, roles, limits and criteria for a rehearsal.
  2. Students will be able to respond to one challenge before asking for feedback.
  3. Students will be able to check feedback against the criteria or a trusted source and identify an improvement.
  4. Students will be able to use that improvement in a fresh attempt and explain what changed.

Key vocabulary

Practice Mode · Rehearsal · Debrief · The human finish

Materials

Student starter prompt for Practice Mode · Five Mode Studio Student Workbook, Practice section, pp. 29–37 · An approved source and the task criteria

Student starter prompt Let's rehearse [skill]. You play [role]. Give me one challenge at a time and wait for my attempt before you give any feedback.

TimeStepTeacherStudentsSay this
Before class · 5 min in classOpening
Design		Choose a task for this Mode. State what Practice Mode may do, what students must do and which source is approved.Name the task’s limits. Bring: One skill to rehearse, an invented scenario and a sign of a successful attempt.“Today you’ll use Practice Mode. Read the task and point to the thinking that stays with you.”
5 minTeach
Model		Use your saved conversation or the Framework example. Ask for one challenge and make your own attempt. Check a piece of feedback against the criteria, then try a new version.Name one point where the model supported the task and one decision the teacher kept.“Ask one question at a time and wait for my attempt before telling me what to improve.”
2 minTeach
Supply		Hand out the starter prompt, the approved source and the task criteria.Fill in the brackets. Check that the information you share is minimal and approved.“Give it enough context to do the permitted job. What can you leave out?”
20 minPractice
Engage		Circulate. Ask what the learner is keeping, changing or checking. Record the exact wording of a cue that identifies a needed decision.Define the rehearsal and answer a challenge before seeing feedback. Find one justified improvement and use it in a fresh attempt.“Point to the part where you directed the help or decided what to do with the response.”
3 minCheck
Share		Use the approved conversation hand-in and confirm the evidence arrives privately.Turn in the task work and the full, dedicated task conversation.“Open what you handed in. Check that the first prompt, every response and your final turn are there.”
5 min · finish reviewing after classCheck
Review		Use the Practice Mode rubric to review the conversation. Mark the finished cycle, record the observed level and give a specific next action.Explain a key decision and point to the turn or class source that supports it.“Show me the evidence of what you checked and what you chose.”
5 minExit
Exit with the human finish		Collect a one-sentence explanation and use it alongside the work and conversation to plan a next attempt.Write a brief answer pointing to your own work.“What did you change in the second attempt, and how do you know it helped?”

Check for understanding Practice Mode rubric, a separate printable. Record the level and the exact cue, if you gave one.

Misconceptions and reteach

  • “I read the model answer. That counts as a rehearsal.” Say: “Do the skill yourself first so you have an attempt to learn from.” Redo: Ask for a fresh challenge and try it before you see a response or a hint.
