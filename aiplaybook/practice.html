Guided practice in each Mode (Permit)

A suggested pace. Stretch or combine days to fit your class.

At a glance
Time

Approved student tool or mediated practice

Goal

Students use each Mode in a real task with teacher supervision and submit evidence of their decisions.

Learning objectives

Students will be able to:

  • Use a named Mode for an approved purpose on an authentic task.
  • Apply all four AI Core Competencies and complete the human finish.
  • Share the full, dedicated task conversation through the agreed private route.

Key vocabulary

Guided practice. A supervised learning task with a clear boundary, teacher modeling and feedback.

Human finish. The final action the student owns in each Mode.

Mode mark. The teacher’s record of a reviewed practice cycle on the Permit.

Lesson flow

Use an approved tool after checking eligibility and the foundations. Choose a Mode lesson above for a timed session.

  1. TeacherDesignChoose a Mode and a task that leaves the assessed thinking with students.
  2. TeacherModelShow the approved input, one redirect and the human finish.
  3. TeacherSupplyGive a starter prompt, safe sources and a private hand-in route.
  4. StudentsEngageRun the dedicated conversation, make the decisions and check the result.
  5. StudentsShareSubmit the complete task conversation and the work.
  6. TeacherReviewRead the evidence, record any support and give feedback.

Check for understanding

Read the work and full conversation with the rubric on that Mode’s page.

Record the level and exact support. A completed, reviewed cycle can earn a Mode mark under your local practice target. Use the conversation to decide where students need more practice.

For a paper or teacher-mediated rehearsal, record what the student demonstrated and what still needs independent or live evidence.

Use Mode Practice (MW-00) to choose the matching printable activity. Keep the Five Mode Studio Teacher Guide and keys with your teacher materials.

Misconceptions and reteach

A long conversation earns a mark

Look at one redirect, one material check and the student’s finish. Model that move again if the evidence is thin.

One helpful suggestion proves an independent choice

Ask what the student decided and why. Capture the exact support and try a new case after feedback.

Hand in the full conversation

Use the route your school approved. Submit the full, dedicated task conversation, including prompts and replies. Keep public chat links out of class submissions.

Any tool

Any school-approved account

Your teacher receives the file through the private class submission. Follow your school’s rules for storing and deleting local copies.

  1. Open the entire task conversation. Expand any folded messages and scroll from the first prompt through the last reply.
  2. Use your browser’s Print menu and choose Save as PDF. Open the PDF and check that it contains both sides of the conversation, from start to finish.
  3. If the browser leaves out turns, copy the whole task conversation into an approved document. Label each turn Student or AI, export it as a PDF, and check for omissions.
  4. Upload the checked file to the private assignment in your class system. Follow your teacher’s directions for any approved attachments needed to understand it.

Gemini

School-managed Google Workspace

If your school enables Drive-based sharing, the settings you choose control who can see the snapshot. Your teacher must have permission to open it.

  1. Open the task chat in Gemini on the web with your school account. Open the chat’s Share menu and choose the Drive-based sharing option if your school provides it.
  2. Keep General access Restricted. Add only your teacher’s school email and set access to Viewer.
  3. Submit the restricted link in the class assignment. If only Create public link is offered, use the private PDF route. The teacher should be able to review the whole conversation.

Personal account

Gemini’s public conversation link can be opened and reshared by anyone who has it.

  1. Open your completed task chat and check all turns are visible.
  2. Use Any tool above to save the full chat to a PDF and submit it privately.

ChatGPT

School-managed ChatGPT workspace

Eligible members of the same Business, Enterprise or Edu workspace can open a workspace link. It may include messages added later. Keep this chat dedicated to the assignment.

  1. Open the task conversation in the school workspace and select Share.
  2. Read the sharing notice and check the full conversation preview. If your teacher approves the workspace link and has access to that workspace, select Create link or Copy link.
  3. Paste the link in the private assignment. If access is uncertain or the teacher needs a fixed copy, use the private PDF route.

Personal account

Anyone who gets a personal shared link can view its snapshot.

  1. Open the completed task conversation and expand any folded messages.
  2. Use Any tool above to save and submit the whole chat as a PDF.

Claude

School-managed account

Only people you invite can open a specific-people share. Your school admin can restrict sharing, including invitations outside the organization.

  1. Open the task chat and choose Share in the upper right.
  2. Under People with access, enter your teacher’s approved email address. Keep access limited to the named recipient and select Send if your school approves this feature.
  3. Follow the class submission directions so the teacher can match the invited snapshot to the assignment. If the option is disabled, use the private PDF route.

Personal account

Use specific-people sharing only if your teacher approved it for personal accounts. A public link grants access to anyone who has it.

  1. Open the task chat and choose Share. Under People with access, enter your teacher’s approved email address.
  2. Select Send. Your teacher signs in with the invited address to see the snapshot. If the invitation is outside your school’s approved route, use the private PDF route instead.
