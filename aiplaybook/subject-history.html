Your subject

History.

Exploring the past with AI, without treating invented answers as evidence.

Ground rules

Before you plan anything

Students still

Students interpret sources, build context, make the argument and decide what the evidence actually supports.

Road closed when

Students are independently analyzing a source, recalling facts or building an original historical argument.

Check it by

Check names, dates, quotations and claims against primary sources and reliable secondary sources.

Have students keep

The full conversation, plus source notes, claim-and-evidence links and any generated scenario clearly labeled as invention.

Watch for: AI can invent facts or quotations and make a fake historical voice sound real.

Watch for: Watch for presentism, stereotypes and false balance.

Practice ModeStudent useNew

Rehearse a historical perspective

Use documented evidence to rehearse questions or possible claims, without pretending the generated voice is a real source.

BringRelevant sources, documented facts and clear limits on what can be inferred.

Suggestion · Worked brief

The museum label

Task
Write a 150-word museum label and a one-minute audio-guide script for one primary source from the unit, for visitors your age.
Your only source
Use this invented extract from the class document packet and its source note. In a fictional town council record dated 18 May 1911, a market vendor asks for a roof to keep produce dry. A council member replies that paving the entrances should come first because delivery carts get stuck after rain. The minutes give the requests but record no decision.
AI is permitted for
  • IdeaCompare angles a curator might take.
  • LearningFill in context or a term, checked against the packet.
  • FeedbackFind where the label claims more than the source shows.
  • PracticeRehearse answering visitor questions.
  • ToolTurn your notes into a timeline strip for the display.

Use each AI Mode purposefully. Choose your own prompts and order.

Yours and assessed
Choose a historically defensible interpretation, attribute both views and say what the minutes leave unresolved. Write the label and script yourself. Check each factual claim against the extract and source note.
Assessed separately
History: sourcing, interpretation, accuracy and acknowledgment of the limits of the record. Review responsible AI use separately.
Hand in
A 150-word museum label and a one-minute audio-guide script, the full, dedicated task conversation, and an explanation of what you accepted or rejected, what you checked and how you developed the work.
Privacy
Use approved task material and your own work. Keep names, identifiable images and personal information out of the tool. Submit through your private class route.
