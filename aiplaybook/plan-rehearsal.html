AI Production Teacher Playbook

Practice a whole project

Printable lesson plan

Goal. Use the five AI Modes together on one practice project and get feedback before the License assessment. Time. 45–60 minutes, plus feedback and a retry as needed

Learning objectives

  • Start with your own work and pick the order that fits your task.
  • Give each AI Mode a job that helps your project. Make the important choices yourself.
  • Check an important claim or change and explain how you checked it.
  • Hand in your work, the whole chat for the assignment, and a short note about your choices.

Materials

  • Classroom kit rehearsal L1-R1 (student packet, p. 3) and the teacher model LT-03 (License Assessment Guide, p. 3).
  • A repeatable practice brief in your subject, approved sources and the usual rules for AI and private submission.
  • The student practice sheet. Use the storyboard example or replace it with a similar brief in your class.
