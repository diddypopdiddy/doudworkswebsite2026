Practice Mode in the Framework

Goal

Use AI to set up a rehearsal, attempt the skill yourself and use checked feedback to improve.

  1. Set upDefine the situation, roles and limits
  2. Use AIAttempt the simulation
  3. Think it throughCheck feedback for strengths and gaps
  4. The human finishImprove and retry until the goal is met

Learning objectives

  1. Students will be able to set up a scenario, roles, limits and criteria for a rehearsal.
  2. Students will be able to respond to one challenge before asking for feedback.
  3. Students will be able to check feedback against the criteria or a trusted source and identify an improvement.
  4. Students will be able to use that improvement in a fresh attempt and explain what changed.

Key vocabulary

Practice Mode
Use AI to set up a rehearsal, attempt the skill yourself and use checked feedback to improve.
Rehearsal
A chance to perform a skill and improve before it counts.
Debrief
A short look back at what worked and what to try next.
The human finish
The decision, explanation, check or approval you do yourself before using the result.

Task ideas by subject
  • English language artsRehearse the discussion

    Have AI take a reading of the text that disagrees with yours and press you one question at a time. Answer with evidence from the text before it responds.

    Bring Your claim, the text and two pieces of evidence you chose yourself.

  • HistoryRehearse a historical perspective

    Use documented evidence to rehearse questions or possible claims, without pretending the generated voice is a real source.

    Bring Relevant sources, documented facts and clear limits on what can be inferred.

  • MathematicsChallenge the reasoning

    Ask for a counterexample or a challenge question, then defend or revise your original method.

    Bring A method you chose, shown work and why you think it should work.

  • ScienceTry a labeled simulation

    Change one condition, predict what may happen and name what a real experiment would still need to test.

    Bring The concept, known conditions, a prediction and a clear line between model and observation.

  • World languagesRehearse a conversation

    Practice a teacher-approved scenario at your level, then use the key phrases in a real conversation.

    Bring Target words or structures, a clear scenario and a first attempt at a response.

  • Business and computer scienceTest a prototype

    Ask AI for failure cases, unusual users or changing conditions. Predict what will happen, then test the strongest ones.

    Bring A clear user, a few requirements and a working idea or prototype.

  • Physical education and healthSet a goal and reflect

    Use questions to clarify a realistic goal and reflect on progress. Not to get health advice.

    Bring The student's own practice record and teacher-approved goal criteria.

  • Related artsRehearse the critique

    Have AI play an audience member asking one question at a time about your intention and choices. Answer before it responds. It isn't the judge of quality.

    Bring Your work in progress described in your own words, and your intention.

Special education access notes and adult-use ideas

Lesson flow

The six steps of guided practice, and what Practice Mode changes in four of them.

  1. TeacherDesignPick the AI Mode and the task.
  2. TeacherModelShow how you direct the help.
  3. TeacherSupplyHand out a starter prompt.
  4. StudentsEngageDirect the conversation and do the work.
  5. StudentsShareHand in the full, dedicated task conversation.
  6. TeacherReviewRead, discuss and record the mark.

Design · students bring

One skill to rehearse, an invented scenario and a sign of a successful attempt.

Model · show this

Ask for one challenge and make your own attempt. Check a piece of feedback against the criteria, then try a new version.

Say thisAsk one question at a time and wait for my attempt before telling me what to improve.

Engage · students do this

Define the rehearsal and answer a challenge before seeing feedback. Find one justified improvement and use it in a fresh attempt.

Exit · ask this

AskWhat did you change in the second attempt, and how do you know it helped?

Supply, Share and Review work the same way in every AI Mode. See all six steps, or print the full plan for this session.
