Your subject

Physical education and health.

AI for planning and reflection, while movement, safety and health decisions stay human.

One idea per ModeFor your prepLicense examples

Ground rules

Before you plan anything

Students still

Students move, practice, reflect and set their own goals. You observe, coach and make every safety and health decision.

Road closed when

The task involves movement performance, live observation, safety, health advice or relationship-based coaching.

Check it by

Check trusted health guidance, local safety rules and what actually happens in the gym or classroom.

Have students keep

The full conversation, plus practice records, reflections and changes made in the real space.

Watch for: AI can give generic or unsafe health advice.

Watch for: No identifiable health, disability, fitness or behavior information in a public tool.

Guided practice · Module 3

One idea for each Mode

Each one is a single guided-practice cycle. Adapt the task to your unit.

Idea ModeStudent useNew

Design a game variation

Ask for three ways to change a game or warm-up for today's skill focus, with a tradeoff for each. Pick one, fit it to the real space and explain the choice.

BringThe skill focus, group size, equipment and space.

Learning ModeStudent useNew

Understand the why behind a skill

Ask for a plain explanation of a class concept, say it back, then check it against class materials. For concepts, not personal health questions.

BringWhat you already think and the class resource to check against.

Feedback ModeStudent useNew

Check a plan against the criteria

Share your written practice plan or routine design. Ask for gaps against the criteria. Your teacher approves any change before you try it.

BringThe student's written plan and teacher-approved criteria.

Practice ModeStudent useNew

Set a goal and reflect

Use questions to clarify a realistic goal and reflect on progress. Not to get health advice.

BringThe student's own practice record and teacher-approved goal criteria.

Tool ModeStudent useNew

Turn a practice log into a chart

Have AI lay out your activity log as a table or chart. Check it against the log. Activity counts and times only.

BringAn activity log with no health or body data.

License · Module 4

License examples

Suggestions to adapt for your unit. Give students a fresh assessment task after any guided rehearsal.

Suggested worked brief

Design a skill station

Task
Design a 10-minute skill station for classmates on a skill from the unit, with a setup diagram, instructions and safety notes. Then run it.
Only source
Use the teacher’s fictional station card and unit safety rules. Eight learners share one 6 × 8 m area with four foam balls and four cones. The skill is an underhand toss to a floor target. Allow space behind the line, retrieve only on signal and give an accessible seated option. The teacher checks the setup before anyone starts.
What AI is permitted to do
Use the approved tool and source. Use and document all five Modes in the order that fits your task. The options below show where help could fit. Choose your own prompts.
  • Idea ModeCompare variations for the station.
  • Learning ModeUnderstand why a cue works.
  • Feedback ModeCheck the plan against the criteria.
  • Practice ModeRehearse giving the instructions.
  • Tool ModeLay out a one-page station card.
What stays yours
The design, the cues they choose and running it live. You approve safety before anyone uses it.
Subject learning assessed separately
A safe, clear setup, appropriate skill cues, realistic use of space and time, and effective teaching of the station in person.
What to hand in
The finished work, the full, dedicated task conversation, and a short explanation of what each Mode contributed, what you accepted or rejected, and what you verified.
Privacy
Use the fictional setup. Keep health information, accommodations tied to named students and photos or recordings of classmates out of the tool.
Fresh transfer task
Adapt the station on the spot for a different space or group size.

Another starting point

The health claim check

Pick one claim from a set of made-up ads or posts. Make a short poster or talk on what the class sources do and don't support.

Source
Class health sources and a claim set you write.
What stays with the student
The verdict, every line and sorting each claim: supported, contradicted or not established.
