Your subject

Physical education and health.

AI for planning and reflection, while movement, safety and health decisions stay human.

Ground rules

Before you plan anything

Students still

Students move, practice, reflect and set their own goals. You observe, coach and make every safety and health decision.

Road closed when

The task involves movement performance, live observation, safety, health advice or relationship-based coaching.

Check it by

Check trusted health guidance, local safety rules and what actually happens in the gym or classroom.

Have students keep

The full conversation, plus practice records, reflections and changes made in the real space.

Watch for: AI can give generic or unsafe health advice.

Watch for: No identifiable health, disability, fitness or behavior information in a public tool.

Design a game variation

Ask for three ways to change a game or warm-up for today's skill focus, with a tradeoff for each. Pick one, fit it to the real space and explain the choice.

License examples

Adapt these suggestions to your unit and sources.

Suggestion · Worked brief

Design a skill station

Task
Design a 10-minute skill station for classmates on a skill from the unit, with a setup diagram, instructions and safety notes. Then run it.
Your only source
Use the unit’s underhand-toss station card. It asks students to face a floor target, swing underhand and follow through toward the target. Equipment: four soft beanbags, two floor hoops and cones. Only one student tosses in each marked lane. Everyone waits behind the line and collects equipment after the teacher’s signal.
AI is permitted for
  • IdeaCompare variations for the station.
  • LearningUnderstand why a cue works.
  • FeedbackCheck the plan against the criteria.
  • PracticeRehearse giving the instructions.
  • ToolLay out a one-page station card.

Use each AI Mode purposefully. Choose your own prompts and order.

Yours and assessed
Choose a setup and two ways to adjust difficulty, write your own cues and explain your choices. Check the plan against the unit safety rules and get the teacher’s approval before running the station.
Assessed separately
Physical education and health: clear cues, useful practice, accessible adjustments, safe setup and live instruction. Review responsible AI use separately.
Hand in
Your setup diagram and station card, plus your in-class delivery after the teacher approves the plan, the full, dedicated task conversation, and an explanation of what you accepted or rejected, what you checked and how you developed the work.
Privacy
Use approved task material and your own work. Keep names, identifiable images and personal information out of the tool. Submit through your private class route.
