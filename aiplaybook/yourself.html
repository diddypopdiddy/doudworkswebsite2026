Module 0 · Day 0

Use it yourself first

A suggested pace. Stretch or combine days to fit your class.

MaterialsLesson flowCheck for understanding
At a glance
Time
One planning period (45 min)
Live tool
Approved staff tool only

Goal

Know how each Mode works for you and where the tool falls short in your subject before you teach it.

Learning objectives

You will be able to:

  1. Use each of the five AI Modes on your own planning.
  2. Name the human finish for each Mode.
  3. Identify one wrong, vague or overconfident response in your subject.
  4. Save five classroom-safe conversations to model from.

Key vocabulary

AI Mode
The type of support you ask AI to provide.
Human finish
The decision, explanation, revision, retry or approval you own.
Verification
Checking something important against a trusted source or a test.

Materials

  • One upcoming lesson and a trusted course source. Use approved materials and fictional or fully de-identified examples.
  • The five AI Modes. Review the role and human finish for each.
  • Use the five prompts under Lesson flow with your approved staff account.

Lesson flow

Run this preparation module on your own before you model AI use for students.

  1. 5 min

    Opening

    Teacher. Choose a lesson you know well. Name the learning goal and an authentic planning problem.

    You. List the useful materials you have permission to share. Keep student records and individualized information in school systems.

    Say thisI know the learning target. I decide which help fits.
  2. 7 min

    Teach

    Teacher. Read the five AI Mode descriptions. Plan one bounded request for each.

    You. Identify what you will decide, explain, revise, retry or verify at the end of each conversation.

    Say thisWhat thinking will I still need to do when the tool stops?
  3. 20 min

    Practice

    Teacher. Run one of the prompts below for each Mode. Keep each example in its own chat.

    You. Use the replies to make and explain a real planning decision. Keep grading, accommodations and consequential decisions with you.

    Say thisI’ll give it the goal, the boundaries and enough approved context to help.
  4. 8 min

    Check

    Teacher. Ask about something you know cold in your subject. Compare the answer with a trusted source, a computation or a worked example.

    You. Save a short example of an error, assumption or gap. Review any resource for fit, accuracy, accessibility and privacy before students see it.

    Say thisWhich part can I verify? What did the tool assume?
  5. 5 min

    Exit

    Teacher. Choose one conversation to model for each Mode.

    You. Remove identifying details and keep approved teaching copies in the school system.

    Say thisHere’s where I redirected the help and what I did myself.

Check for understanding

Teacher self-check. Answer yes or no before introducing live use.

  • I used all five AI Modes on my own planning.
  • I can point to the human finish for each Mode.
  • I tested one answer about my subject against something I trust.
  • I saved five usable conversations that are safe to show.
  • I know the approved tools and submission route for my class.

Misconceptions and reteach

A neat draft can still have a gap

Point to the answer that looked strongest. Test a material claim or operation and compare it with the original source.

Each Mode needs a human finish

If the conversation ends with a chatbot reply, reopen it and model the choice, explanation or verification you made.

Prompts for your own planning

Idea Mode Open a lesson three ways

I teach [course and grade]. Next week’s lesson is on [concept] and the goal is [learning goal]. Give me three genuinely different ways to open it, with one tradeoff for each. Leave the plan to me.
Human finish. Pick one, adapt it and say why it fits this class.

Learning Mode Get up to speed on a topic

Teach me [topic] through questions. Start by checking what I already know. Give one explanation at a time, then ask me to restate it before you continue.
Human finish. Say it in your own words, then check it against a source you trust.

Feedback Mode Stress-test something you made

Here is my [lesson / rubric / task sheet] and what it is meant to do: [paste approved material]. Point to two gaps and explain what each could mean for students. Leave the rewriting to me.
Human finish. Make the change yourself or explain why you kept it.

Practice Mode Rehearse a hard moment

Let’s rehearse. I need to explain [concept] to a student. Play a fictional student who is confused. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer before giving feedback.
Human finish. Identify the fix and try it again. Use an invented scenario.

Tool Mode Format what you already wrote

Put these notes into a station-rotation table. Use only what’s here. Mark missing items MISSING.
Human finish. Compare it with your notes line by line before students see it.
