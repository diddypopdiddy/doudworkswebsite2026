Module 0 · Day 0 · your prep

Use it yourself first

A suggested pace. Stretch or combine days to fit your class.

Total time
45-minute planning period
Live student tool
None. Approved staff tool for prep.
Five AI Modes in the Framework

Goal

Know how each AI Mode works for you, and where the tool falls short in your subject, before you teach it.

Learning objectives

  1. You will be able to use each of the five AI Modes once on your own planning.
  2. You will be able to name the human finish in each AI Mode.
  3. You will be able to find and document one error or assumption in your subject.
  4. You will be able to save conversations you can use to model the AI Modes.

Key vocabulary

AI Modes
Five roles AI can play in a task: Idea, Learning, Feedback, Practice and Tool.
The human finish
The decision, explanation, check or approval you do yourself before using the result.
The full, dedicated task conversation
The entire exchange for one task, from the first prompt through the finish.

Lesson flow

This is your planning period. Save examples you can safely show when you model each AI Mode in Module 3.

Opening

  1. 5 min

    Choose work you already understand

    Teacher
    Pick a lesson you will teach soon. Use the school-approved staff tool and material approved for that tool.
    Your record
    Write down the course, learning goal, available time and material you can use safely.
    Say thisI’m planning for this class. AI can support a task, and I’m responsible for the result.

Teach

  1. 7 min

    Decide what you will hand off

    Teacher
    Identify the small job the tool can do. Keep student decisions and records with you.
    Your record
    Mark the material you can share and name the part you must do yourself.
    Say thisIf a detail is missing, flag it. I’ll supply it or leave it out.
    What’s fine to hand off, what stays with you and what stays private

    Fine to hand off

    • Organize your notes or draft examples and questions for you to check.
    • Adapt your materials for accessibility. Surface possible misconceptions to investigate.

    Stays with you

    • Make decisions about grades, feedback that counts, accommodations and discipline.
    • Keep relationships with students and families yours.

    Keep private

    • Keep student names, grades, contact details, health, disability and IEP information out of the tool.
    • Avoid private records and stories detailed enough to identify a student.

Practice

  1. 20 min

    Run one prompt in each of the five AI Modes

    Teacher
    Fill in the brackets. Use a separate, dedicated conversation for each task. Redirect the help when it drifts.
    Your record
    Save all five conversations and write a short note on the human finish in each.
    Say thisHere’s the goal, what you can use and where your role ends. Wait for me to make the final decision.

    Idea Mode Open a lesson three ways

    I teach [course and grade]. Next week's lesson is on [concept] and the goal is [learning goal]. Give me three genuinely different ways to open it, with one tradeoff for each. Don't write the full plan.
    The human finish Pick one, adapt it and say why it fits this class.

    Learning Mode Get up to speed on a topic

    Teach me [topic] through questions. Start by checking what I already know. Give one explanation or example at a time, then ask me to restate it before you continue.
    The human finish Say it back in your own words, then check it against a source you trust.

    Feedback Mode Stress-test something you made

    Here is my [lesson / rubric / task sheet] and what it's meant to do: [paste]. Point to the two biggest gaps and explain the effect of each on students. Don't rewrite it.
    The human finish Make the change yourself, or keep it and know why.

    Practice Mode Rehearse a hard moment

    Let's rehearse. I need to explain [concept or decision] to [a confused student / a worried parent]. You play that person. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer before you give any feedback.
    The human finish Try it again with the fix. Made-up scenario only. No real names.

    Tool Mode Format what you already wrote

    Turn these notes into [a station-rotation table / a checklist / student directions]. Use only what's here. If something is missing, write MISSING instead of guessing.
    The human finish Compare it with your notes line by line before students see it.

Check

  1. 8 min

    Find where the tool falls short

    Teacher
    Ask a question in your subject that you know cold. Compare the response to an approved source or a result you can reproduce. Mark where it is wrong, vague or overconfident.
    Your record
    Save the exact claim, your check and the correction. If the first response holds up, test a common exception or missing detail. Use the example in Module 1.
    Say thisWhich part can I check myself? What should I hold back until I have evidence?

Exit

  1. 5 min

    Check it before students see it

    Teacher
    Check facts, examples, calculations, accessibility, bias and tone against your curriculum and class. Revise or discard anything that misses the goal.
    Your record
    Choose a conversation to model. Complete the self-check below.
    Say thisI’ll show where I redirected the tool and what I did to finish the work.

Check for understanding

Teacher self-check

Answer yes or no for each statement.

I used each of the five AI Modes once in my planning.
I can name the human finish for every AI Mode I tried.
I recorded at least one error, unsupported assumption or vague answer and how I checked it.
I saved five conversations that I can safely model from.
I checked the material I plan to put in front of students.

For any no, return to the relevant step before you model it.

Misconceptions and reteach

“I can use the plan as soon as it reads well.”
Say thisCheck what it asks students to learn and whether the examples hold up.

Redo Compare one example line by line with your source and fix it yourself.

“I took the student’s name off. It’s ready to paste.”
Say thisClass details can identify someone. Use a fictional example with only what the task needs.

Redo Replace the record with a short, invented scenario.
