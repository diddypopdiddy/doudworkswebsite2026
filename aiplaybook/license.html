Module 4 · 1–2 periods

The License assessment

A suggested pace. Stretch or combine days to fit your class.

MaterialsLesson flowCheck for understanding
At a glance
Time
1–2 class periods, plus review
Live tool
School-approved student tool

Goal

Students show they can use AI independently within the limits of an open task in your subject.

Learning objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. Engage and document all five AI Modes with a purpose for each.
  2. Keep authorship and demonstrate the assigned learning through the finished work.
  3. Explain material checks, accepted and rejected help, and the decisions you own.
  4. Apply the same competencies on a fresh transfer task.

Key vocabulary

Open-AI assessment
An assessment with approved AI and resources available inside stated limits.
Purposeful Mode use
Choosing AI help for a real need in the task and carrying out the human finish.
Dedicated task conversation
A complete exchange that covers this task only, from the first prompt to the last reply.
Fresh transfer task
A short new case that tests whether a student can apply the learning in a different situation.

Materials

  • One task brief, an approved source and the usual subject rubric.
  • The Framework assessment overview and your classroom kit’s License Assessment Guide (LT-04 and LT-05).
  • Private hand-in instructions for the approved account or a full PDF.
  • Worked examples by subject to adapt before class.

Lesson flow

Run this as an open-AI assessment, like an open-book test. Once guided practice is complete, give students an open-ended task in your subject.

Students choose their own prompts and use and document all five AI Modes for real purposes. They decide the sequence and how much each Mode contributes, within the task’s limits.

  1. 5 min

    Opening

    Teacher. Hand out the brief. Review the approved source, tool, permitted actions, retained thinking and hand-in route.

    Students. Summarize the task and name one decision you must make yourself.

    Say thisThe work is yours. Show which type of help served a purpose and how you checked it.
  2. 7 min

    Teach

    Teacher. Review the three things students submit. Clarify the evidence and access route without walking through the assessment case for them.

    Students. Explain how the finished work, conversation and explanation will show your process.

    Say thisKeep one full, dedicated task conversation. Be ready to explain what you accepted, rejected and verified.
  3. 30–60 min

    Practice

    Teacher. Give time for the independent task. Record any cue you provide and make approved accommodations available.

    Students. Use all five Modes where they fit, document the work, check important outputs and submit the finished work, full conversation and explanation.

    Say thisUse your own prompts. Choose the order that fits your task.
  4. 10 min + review

    Check

    Teacher. Review subject work and AI-use evidence against their own criteria. Use a short fresh transfer task with a new example or constraint.

    Students. Explain one decision without AI, then do the fresh transfer task using the permission you were given.

    Say thisShow what you can carry into a new case.
  5. 5 min

    Exit

    Teacher. Record Ready, Needs another attempt or Pause after reviewing the evidence. Give the student the next step.

    Students. Name one action you’ll repeat or improve on the next assignment.

    Say thisI’m making a decision from your evidence and what you can show on your own.

Check for understanding

License evidence
CriterionWhat to look for
Purposeful Mode useFind the purpose of each Mode and the decisions the student can explain.
PromptingCheck the approved context, the requested role and how the student redirected.
Data & PrivacyCheck that the input, tool and submission route were approved and kept to the minimum needed.
EthicsCheck the task boundary, disclosed help and ownership of the assessed thinking.
VerificationCheck claimed sources, factual claims and important operations against evidence.

Use the kit rubric levels Not yet, With guidance and Independent. Record the exact cue if you gave one. Assess the subject learning with your usual rubric.

Give a fresh transfer task before you make the License decision.

Ready

The student shows the learning target, documents all five Modes, explains their choices, follows permission and privacy rules, and verifies important results.

Needs another attempt

Name the gap. Offer targeted guided practice, then review fresh evidence.

Pause

Pause live use if eligibility, data permission or task permission is unresolved. Follow school procedure before resuming.

Misconceptions and reteach

A strong product can hide a missing Mode

Ask which Mode helped and where the evidence shows it. Give practice in the missing Mode and check a new example.

I verified it with the same chatbot

Select one material claim or operation. Have the student verify it against an approved source, a test or a separate calculation.
