Earning the License in the Framework

Goal

Students show they can use AI independently on an open task.

  1. What it is An open-AI assessment, like an open-book test, after guided practice.
  2. What students do Complete an open-ended task. Use and document each AI Mode to some degree for a real purpose, with your own prompts and in any order.
  3. What they submit The finished work, the full, dedicated task conversation and an explanation. See the conversation hand-in steps.
  4. What you look for Purposeful AI Mode use and the four AI Core Competencies. Assess subject learning separately.
  5. Fresh transfer task In class, use a new brief and source. Ask which AI Modes fit and have the learner use one live while you observe.
  6. Your decision Ready, Needs another attempt or Pause.

Learning objectives

  1. Students will be able to choose and document a purpose for all five AI Modes within an open-ended task.
  2. Students will be able to apply the four AI Core Competencies while keeping the subject thinking and the human finish their own.
  3. Students will be able to explain what they accepted, rejected and verified using evidence from the work and conversation.
  4. Students will be able to show how they would apply the same skills to a fresh transfer task.

Key vocabulary

License
The teacher’s decision that you can use AI independently in this class within each assignment’s limits.
Purposeful AI Mode use
Each AI Mode does a real supporting job and you can explain why you used it.
The full, dedicated task conversation
All turns for this one task, kept as evidence of how AI helped.
Fresh transfer task
A new situation where you show you can apply the same skills.

Lesson flow

Allow one class period for a short open task and use a second period for the fresh transfer task or a longer brief.

Opening

  1. 5 min

    Launch the task

    Teacher
    Hand out a fresh, open-ended brief and one approved source. State the assessed thinking, permitted actions, privacy limits and approved hand-in method.
    Students
    Read the brief and explain one decision the task reserves for you.
    Say thisUse every AI Mode for a reason. You choose the order and write your own prompts.

Teach

  1. 5 min

    Clarify the directions

    Teacher
    Answer procedural questions and provide needed access supports such as read-aloud directions, larger text, a scribe or extra time. Record any coaching that identifies a content decision.
    Students
    Identify the work you’ll produce and the evidence you’ll submit. Ask about any unclear permission.
    Say thisWhat’s your source? What do you need permission to share?

Practice

  1. 25–45 min

    Complete the open task

    Teacher
    Observe. Keep the assessed decisions with the student. If you give a content cue, record exactly what it was.
    Students
    Use all five AI Modes within the task’s limits. Check material claims and operations against your source. Keep the whole conversation and finish the work yourself.
    Say thisKeep a record of how each AI Mode helped and which decisions you made.

Check

  1. 5 min

    Collect the work and conversation

    Teacher
    Use your approved private hand-in route. Check that the conversation runs from the opening prompt through the human finish.
    Students
    Submit the finished work, the full, dedicated task conversation and your explanation of what you accepted, rejected and checked.
    Say thisShow me enough of your process to explain how you got to the finished work.
  2. About 20 min

    Give the fresh transfer task

    Teacher
    Use a new brief and approved source during class. Ask the student to explain which AI Modes fit and have them complete one live. Keep the case for teacher use.
    Students
    Explain which AI Modes would help and why. Use one and show what you checked.
    Say thisWhat would you do here? Which decision stays with you?

Exit

  1. 5 min

    Explain a decision from the work

    Teacher
    Ask each learner to name a purposeful use and one piece of evidence for it. Review the rubric and record your decision after you have all the evidence.
    Students
    Point to a turn where you accepted, rejected, redirected or verified something. Explain why.
    Say thisFind a choice you made and show how it affected what you turned in.

Check for understanding

The License rubric

One rubric per student, about two sheets. It has a row for each of the five AI Modes and each of the four AI Core Competencies, with the classroom kit’s wording. Assess the subject work with its own criteria.

  • Purposeful AI Mode use What each of the five AI Modes contributed and how the student kept the decisions.
  • Prompting Fitting direction, context and repairs when needed.
  • Data & Privacy The student shares only minimal approved information and stops when unsure.
  • Ethics The student follows permission, owns the work and explains AI’s help honestly.
  • Verification The student checks important claims and operations, and fixes or withholds unsupported results.

Levels Not yet · With guidance · Independent. Record any cue that identified a needed decision as With guidance.

Printable License rubric

Ready

They show the learning target, explain their choices, document every AI Mode, follow permission and privacy rules, and verify results. Record the scope of the License for this class.

Say thisYou can use AI on the assignments I open, within the limits I give you. I can still ask to see the work and conversation.

Needs another attempt

Name the specific gap. Practice it and check fresh evidence.

Say thisHere’s the part to practice. Then we’ll look at fresh evidence together.

Pause

Stop live use when tool eligibility, data permission or assignment permission is not met. Follow school procedure before resuming.

Say thisPause live use for now. I need to check what’s approved before we continue.

Misconceptions and reteach

“I need five AI Modes, so I’ll add one extra prompt for each.”
Say thisEach AI Mode needs a real use. What did it help you do, and what did you decide?

Redo Revisit the missing or unconvincing use on a fresh task.

“The finished work is strong, so I’m ready.”
Say thisI’ll review the subject work and your AI use separately. Show me the choices and checks in the conversation.

Redo Ask the student to explain one choice and verify it using a new source.

“Read-aloud directions count as coaching.”
Say thisAccess supports help you reach the same target. A content cue supplies part of a decision.

Redo Record the support separately and gather fresh independent evidence if a content cue was needed.
