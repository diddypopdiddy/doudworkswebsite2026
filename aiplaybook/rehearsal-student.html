AI Production Teacher Playbook

Practice a whole project

Do this with your teacher before the AI Production License assessment.

Make a storyboard that teaches a beginner

Make three panels that show when a wide shot helps and when a close-up helps. Write your own short narration. If your teacher gives you a different project, follow that brief.

Class source. A wide shot shows where people or objects are in the scene. A close-up makes a detail easier to see. These notes give no rule for how long each shot must last.

What to do

  1. Write or sketch a first plan yourself. Check the assignment rules and use only information your teacher says you may share.
  2. Use all five AI Modes where each one helps. You pick the order and write the prompts. You might compare ideas, ask about a shot, get comments on your draft, rehearse an explanation, or format your own shot list.
  3. Use your source or a test to check important claims and changes the tool made. Make the choice, change, or final approval yourself.
  4. Try a short new example your teacher gives you. Use the rules for that example.

What to hand in

  • Your first plan and finished work.
  • The whole chat for this assignment, from your first message to the last reply. Use the private place your teacher gives you.
  • A short Evidence Log. For each Mode, point to a message and explain why you used it. Include what you checked and what you kept, changed, or left out.
