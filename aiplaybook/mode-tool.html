Tool Mode in the Framework

Goal

Direct a specific operation, then verify and approve the result yourself.

  1. Set upSpecify the task and result
  2. Use AIDirect AI’s work
  3. Think it throughCheck and refine
  4. The human finishVerify and approve the result

Learning objectives

  1. Students will be able to specify a task, the result they need and what must stay the same.
  2. Students will be able to direct a bounded operation using only approved input.
  3. Students will be able to compare the output with the input and repair material discrepancies.
  4. Students will be able to verify and approve the supported result or withhold unresolved parts.

Key vocabulary

Tool Mode
Direct a specific operation, then verify and approve the result yourself.
Operation
A specific action, such as formatting a table or sorting rows.
Source
The original material you compare with the result.
The human finish
The decision, explanation, check or approval you do yourself before using the result.

Task ideas by subject
  • English language artsOrganize your own notes

    Turn your annotations into an outline or evidence chart using only what you wrote. Compare it line by line with your notes. Nothing gets added.

    Bring Your own annotations and the format you want.

  • HistoryBuild a timeline from your notes

    Turn your own source notes into a timeline or comparison table. Check every name and date against the sources. Missing stays blank.

    Bring Your notes, with the source marked for each entry.

  • MathematicsTidy your own data

    Have AI lay out data you collected as a clean table. Compare every row with the original, keep missing values and work out the totals yourself.

    Bring Your own data and the layout you want.

  • ScienceFormat your data table

    Organize raw measurements into a clean table with units. Missing values stay missing. Work out the averages yourself.

    Bring Your raw data, exactly as recorded.

  • World languagesMake a study set from your own list

    Turn your class vocabulary list into flashcards or a quiz. Check every entry against class materials. No new translations.

    Bring The unit vocabulary list.

  • Business and computer scienceClean up code or data

    Reformat student-written code, data or project material without handing over the main design decision. Then test the result.

    Bring Student-created material, the format you want and a way to test it.

  • Physical education and healthTurn a practice log into a chart

    Have AI lay out your activity log as a table or chart. Check it against the log. Activity counts and times only.

    Bring An activity log with no health or body data.

  • Related artsPlan the logistics

    Turn your own rehearsal notes, materials list or shot list into a schedule or checklist. Compare it with your notes. No creative decisions handed over.

    Bring Your notes and the format you want.

Special education access notes and adult-use ideas

Lesson flow

The six steps of guided practice, and what Tool Mode changes in four of them.

  1. TeacherDesignPick the AI Mode and the task.
  2. TeacherModelShow how you direct the help.
  3. TeacherSupplyHand out a starter prompt.
  4. StudentsEngageDirect the conversation and do the work.
  5. StudentsShareHand in the full, dedicated task conversation.
  6. TeacherReviewRead, discuss and record the mark.

Design · students bring

A small approved source you understand and a format or operation you can check.

Model · show this

Format a short set of notes with one missing entry. Compare every row with the original. Ask for a repair if a value changes and check again.

Say thisChange the format. Keep all the original facts and mark anything the source doesn’t supply.

Engage · students do this

Name what can change and what stays. Run the operation, compare the result with the source, then verify and approve or hold it back.

Exit · ask this

AskWhich check let you approve the result? What did you do with anything missing?

Supply, Share and Review work the same way in every AI Mode. See all six steps, or print the full plan for this session.

With guidance means a cue identifies the decision the student needs to make. Record the exact cue. Permitted AI help can still be part of independent work. The student must do the thinking being assessed.

Misconceptions and reteach

Say thisCheck what’s in the table against your original material.

Redo Trace each row back to the source, including a missing or changed value.
