Module 3 · Five sessions · Permit

Tool Mode

A suggested pace. Stretch or combine days to fit your class.

MaterialsLesson flowCheck for understanding
At a glance
Time
45 minutes
Live tool
School-approved student tool

Goal

Practice Tool Mode on a real task and show the human finish in the work and conversation.

Learning objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. Specify the task and expected result and name what stays yours.
  2. Direct AI’s work within limits within the task’s limits.
  3. Check and refine using the approved source or criteria.
  4. Verify and approve the result in your own work.

Key vocabulary

Tool Mode
An AI Mode with four steps that leads to a human finish: verify and approve the result.
Operation
The specific support task, such as reorganizing notes or formatting a table.
Human finish
The final decision or action you own and can explain.

Materials

  • Bring a small set of approved notes or values and an output format. Find a task by subject.
  • Framework reference and the Five Mode Studio Student Workbook’s Tool Mode reference (MO-R).
  • Final Boss for an optional warm-up.
  • Approved private submission route and the starter prompt below.

Student starter

Format these approved notes as [output]. Keep every value and its meaning. Mark anything missing MISSING. Use only my source. I’ll compare and approve the result.
Replace the brackets with your approved context and the limits of this task.

Lesson flow

Before class · Design. Choose the task, the approved inputs, and the part of the work students must do themselves.

  1. 5 min

    Opening

    Teacher. Share the task, Mode and limits. Ask students for a first attempt or a starting idea.

    Students. Bring a small set of approved notes or values and an output format.

    Say thisWhat are you asking the tool to do, and what will you have to do yourself?
  2. 7 min

    Teach

    Teacher. Model. Give a bounded formatting job. Compare the output with the original and leave a missing value unresolved. Approve only after you can explain your checks. Supply. Hand out the starter prompt and approved sources.

    Students. Underline the boundary in the starter prompt and personalize it to your own task.

    Say thisWatch where I check the reply and make my own decision.
  3. 20 min

    Practice

    Teacher. Circulate during Engage. Notice where the student directs the help, protects inputs and checks the output.

    Students. Engage. State the operation, output and information to preserve. Compare every important detail to the source, ask for a correction if needed, and approve or withhold the result.

    Say thisPoint to one place you did your own thinking and tell me what changed.
  4. 8 min

    Check

    Teacher. Review. Look for the source, requested operation, verification and your approval decision. Record the evidence and any exact cue you provided.

    Students. Share. Submit the work and the full task conversation. Show the evidence you want me to see.

    Say thisShow me the prompt, what you checked and the action you owned.
  5. 5 min

    Exit

    Teacher. Name one next step. Offer another opportunity if the student needs evidence in a new context.

    Students. Describe your human finish and one move you would repeat or change.

    Say thisWhat would you carry into the next task?

Check for understanding

Read the finished work and the full, dedicated task conversation. Record the human finish and any exact cue you give. With guidance means a cue from you identified the choice or check the student needed. Score the evidence of the student’s decisions. Chat length, polish and AI praise carry no weight.
