Feedback Mode

Printable lesson plan

Goal Get critique on work you’ve developed while keeping the judgment and revision with you.

Learning objectives

  1. Students will be able to supply their own work, its purpose and the relevant criteria.
  2. Students will be able to request feedback about strengths and gaps, with reasons.
  3. Students will be able to evaluate advice against the brief and approved sources.
  4. Students will be able to make and explain their own revision or justify keeping sound work.

Key vocabulary

Feedback Mode · Criterion · Revision · The human finish

Materials

Student starter prompt for Feedback Mode · Five Mode Studio Student Workbook, Feedback section, pp. 20–28 · An approved source and the task criteria

Student starter prompt Here is my draft and the criteria: [paste both]. Point to two gaps and explain the effect of each. Don't rewrite anything.

TimeStepTeacherStudentsSay this
Before class · 5 min in classOpening
Design		Choose a task for this Mode. State what Feedback Mode may do, what students must do and which source is approved.Name the task’s limits. Bring: Your own first attempt or draft, and two criteria from the assignment.“Today you’ll use Feedback Mode. Read the task and point to the thinking that stays with you.”
5 minTeach
Model		Use your saved conversation or the Framework example. Show an approved draft and one criterion. Read a suggestion, compare it with the criterion and write or decline the change yourself.Name one point where the model supported the task and one decision the teacher kept.“I asked for the gap and a reason. I’ll decide what to change and write it myself.”
2 minTeach
Supply		Hand out the starter prompt, the approved source and the task criteria.Fill in the brackets. Check that the information you share is minimal and approved.“Give it enough context to do the permitted job. What can you leave out?”
20 minPractice
Engage		Circulate. Ask what the learner is keeping, changing or checking. Record the exact wording of a cue that identifies a needed decision.Ask for specific gaps with reasons. Check the advice against your brief and sources. Write your own changes and explain one you made or chose to skip.“Point to the part where you directed the help or decided what to do with the response.”
3 minCheck
Share		Use the approved conversation hand-in and confirm the evidence arrives privately.Turn in the task work and the full, dedicated task conversation.“Open what you handed in. Check that the first prompt, every response and your final turn are there.”
5 min · finish reviewing after classCheck
Review		Use the Feedback Mode rubric to review the conversation. Mark the finished cycle, record the observed level and give a specific next action.Explain a key decision and point to the turn or class source that supports it.“Show me the evidence of what you checked and what you chose.”
5 minExit
Exit with the human finish		Collect a one-sentence explanation and use it alongside the work and conversation to plan a next attempt.Write a brief answer pointing to your own work.“Which suggestion improved the draft, or why did you keep what you had?”

Check for understanding Feedback Mode rubric, a separate printable. Record the level and the exact cue, if you gave one.

Misconceptions and reteach

  • “The rewrite sounded smoother, so I pasted it.” Say: “Use the feedback to find the issue. You write the improvement.” Redo: Return to the first draft and revise one part against a criterion.
