Task
Design a 10-minute skill station for classmates on a skill from the unit, with a setup diagram, instructions and safety notes. Then run it.
Your only source
Use the unit’s underhand-toss station card. It asks students to face a floor target, swing underhand and follow through toward the target. Equipment: four soft beanbags, two floor hoops and cones. Only one student tosses in each marked lane. Everyone waits behind the line and collects equipment after the teacher’s signal.
